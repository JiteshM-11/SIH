What every record should tell you

Health, safety, and a passport that travels with the cell.

Health

State of Health, estimated from how the battery actually lived.

  • Charge cycle count & depth of discharge
  • Average charge / discharge rate
  • Temperature exposure over time

Safety

Risk signals read before they become faults.

  • Heat build‑up during fast charging

A verifiable record for reuse or recycling.

  • Tamper‑evident history, tied to the cell
  • Routes to resale, storage reuse, or recycling
  • Readable by resellers, recyclers & regulators
  • Clearly marks estimated vs. certified entries
Stage 01 · Raw materials

From mine to passport

01

Raw materials & cell chemistry

Lithium, nickel, and cobalt (or iron‑phosphate) are refined into electrode material and wound or stacked into the cell format the pack calls for.

02

Cell assembly & formation

Cells are formed, aged, and graded. This is where CellPass's baseline diagnostic record begins — the day‑zero reference every later reading compares against.

03

Pack & vehicle integration

Cells are welded into modules, wrapped in a battery management system, and mounted into the vehicle chassis with thermal and safety monitoring wired in.

04

On‑road life, then passport

Every charge, trip and season updates the estimate. When the battery leaves the vehicle, its full passport — estimated and certified entries alike — travels with it.
